YouTube-Faceless-Channels

A private publishing tool. It uploads finished explainer videos to its owner's own YouTube channels and sets their title, description, thumbnail, schedule and playlist.

The tool is run by one person on one computer. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

The channels it publishes to

  • Plain Planet
  • Science Wink
  • Open Manual
  • Clackonomics
